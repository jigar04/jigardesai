PaperPilot

Upload research PDFs, ask questions and get answers with citations that point to the exact section and page. It also writes structured summaries and compares findings across papers.

The problem

Reading one paper takes hours and a literature review needs dozens. Pasting PDFs into a general chatbot often gives made-up claims, no page references, and breaks on long documents or several papers at once.

What I built

  • Ingestion: parse PDFs with PyMuPDF, detect sections, split into section-aware chunks with overlap, embed locally with a BGE model, and store in ChromaDB with title, section and page.
  • Answering: rewrite follow-up questions into standalone queries, retrieve the top chunks, answer with numbered citations, then check every cited number against the retrieved passages.
  • Summaries: five fixed questions (problem, method, data, results, limitations) each answered from its own retrieved evidence.
  • Comparison: retrieve per paper so one long paper cannot crowd out the others, then produce a table with citations in each cell.
  • A relevance threshold and a “not found” fallback stop it answering from weak matches.

Stack

  • Python
  • PyMuPDF
  • sentence-transformers
  • ChromaDB
  • FastAPI
  • Streamlit
  • pytest
